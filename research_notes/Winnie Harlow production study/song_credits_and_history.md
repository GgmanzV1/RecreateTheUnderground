# Nettspend – "Winnie Harlow": credits and release history

Research date: 2026-09-29. Confidence labels: **High** = primary/official source or several agreeing independent sources; **Medium** = one reasonable secondary source or a strong inference; **Low** = fan claim, search-engine summary, or unverified listing.

Access notes: Several key pages blocked automated fetching: X/Twitter (402), the Nettspend Fandom HTML page (402; its wikitext came through the MediaWiki API), Chordify (Cloudflare 403), YouTube watch pages (bot check; the oEmbed metadata still worked), Reddit search (blocked), and tunebat (Cloudflare challenge). Where a fact comes only from a search-engine snippet rather than the page itself, it is marked that way.

## Who produced "Winnie Harlow"? (the "ok" and "KwickedOnAllPlats" credits)

### Takeaway
"Winnie Harlow" was produced by **OK** (styled "ok"), Nettspend's main producer on *Bad Ass F\*cking Kid*. Wikipedia credits him as sole producer, with songwriting credited to Shepardson (Nettspend) and Minnix, and the Audiomack "ok" credit agrees (**High**). OK appears to be William Dale Minnix III, a producer from Charlotte, NC (**Medium-High**). "KwickedOnAllPlats" is **not** a real production credit. It is text from the bio of a small TikTok account (@_kwicked, "KWicked on all plats"), and it most likely got attached to the song through a TikTok instrumental or remake video (**High** that it is not a credit; **Medium** on how it got attached). No co-producer is listed anywhere.

### Cited Findings
- Wikipedia's *Bad Ass F\*cking Kid* track listing has a separate block headed "Webstore exclusive digital deluxe edition". It lists track 16 "Winnie Harlow" at 2:49, writers "Shepardson | Minnix", producer "OK", with total length 33:50 (standard edition 31:01). — [Wikipedia: Bad Ass F*cking Kid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bad_Ass_F*cking_Kid)
- The same Wikipedia listing credits OK on 10 of the 15 standard tracks: "Leader" (with Kenny Beats), "Project Pat", "Tyla", "ASAP", "Skipping Class", "Shut Up", "Birdbox", "Perc Soda" and "Laughin". Each of these has "Minnix" in the writer column, the same writer pairing given for "Winnie Harlow". — [Wikipedia: Bad Ass F*cking Kid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bad_Ass_F*cking_Kid)
- Wikipedia's personnel section (sourced to Tidal) names Anthony Kilhoffer as mixing and mastering engineer for tracks 1–3 and 5–15, Moustafa Moustafa for track 4, and Anand "AJ" Joshi as engineer. It gives **no mix or master credit for track 16 ("Winnie Harlow")**. — [Wikipedia: Bad Ass F*cking Kid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bad_Ass_F*cking_Kid)

### Inferences
- OK, "ok" and "Minnix" almost certainly refer to one person. On Wikipedia, every OK-produced BAFK track, including "Winnie Harlow", has "Minnix" as its only non-Nettspend writer, and "F\*ck Swag" pairs the OK production credit with William Dale Minnix III. Nothing I read states "OK = William Dale Minnix III" in one sentence, so this is **Medium-High** confidence.
- "KwickedOnAllPlats" is almost certainly an **attribution error**. A small TikTok beatmaker's bio text or hashtag ("KWicked on all plats" means "Kwicked on all platforms") was probably attached to a TikTok remake or instrumental of the song, and aggregators then picked it up as a credit. Kwicked is not credited on any official or archive listing, so treat the "KwickedOnAllPlats" credit as **refuted**.
- Because OK is Nettspend's main collaborator (about 10 of the 15 BAFK tracks plus "F\*ck Swag" and "Nothing Like Uuu"), "Winnie Harlow" should be studied as part of OK's BAFK-era production style, alongside "Shut Up", "Birdbox", "Tyla" and similar tracks.

### Gaps
- I found no statement from OK or Nettspend (X, Instagram, livestream or interview) that discusses "Winnie Harlow" specifically.
- I could not confirm OK's legal name from a source that states it outright, and did not open the Muso.ai profile.
- Mixing and mastering credits for track 16 are unknown, since the Tidal-based personnel list covers only tracks 1–15.
- I could not open the specific @_kwicked video that ties the account to "Winnie Harlow" (TikTok pages would not load its caption). Its date and content are unverified.

## Release status, timeline and era

### Takeaway
"Winnie Harlow" is an **official release, not a leak or snippet**. It is the 16th and bonus track on a webstore-exclusive digital edition, *BAD ASS F\*CKING KID (WINNIE VERSION)*, sold on Interscope's store (**High**). The "archive" uploads on SoundCloud and Audiomack are fan re-uploads of that purchasable track, posted on Dec 12 and Dec 14, 2024. It belongs to the BAFK era (standard edition released Dec 6, 2024). No earlier snippet history was found.

### Cited Findings
- Interscope's official store sells "BAD ASS F\*CKING KID (WINNIE VERSION) Digital Mixtape" for $8. It is a digital download delivered by email, limited to one per customer, with 16 tracks, the last being "Winnie Harlow". The page gives no release date or credits. — [Interscope store](https://interscope.com/products/bad-ass-f-cking-kid-winnie-version-digital-mixtape)
- The standard *Bad Ass F\*cking Kid* came out December 6, 2024 on Grade A Productions and Interscope. Wikipedia gives a 2020–2024 recording period and lists the bonus track under a "Webstore exclusive digital deluxe edition" heading. — [Wikipedia: Bad Ass F*cking Kid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bad_Ass_F*cking_Kid)
- The fan account Nettspend Central (@nettcentral) posted "WINNIE HARLOW (Bonus Track)" in status 1867296239752294716. Decoding that X ID gives **2024-12-12 19:51 UTC**. — [X: @nettcentral](https://x.com/nettcentral/status/1867296239752294716)
- The "Nettspend Archive" SoundCloud upload (track ID 1982459479) has these metadata values from the page's embedded data: created **2024-12-12 20:56:45 UTC**, about an hour after the Nettspend Central post. Its uploader-set "release_date" field is **2024-12-08**. Duration is **2:17** (137.2 s). No description, genre or tags. When checked it had about 1.29M plays, 26.1K likes, 494 reposts and 525 comments. The account has 6 tracks and about 6.4K followers. — [SoundCloud: Nettspend Archive](https://soundcloud.com/nettspendarchive/nettspend-winnie-harlow-prod)
- The Audiomack upload by "bloodcore" is dated **December 14, 2024**. Its structured data gives a duration of **PT2M18S (2:18)**. — [Audiomack: bloodcore-1](https://audiomack.com/bloodcore-1/song/winnie-harlow)
- A TikTok post by @poloactivee is captioned "nettspend - winnie harlow [2024]". Its ID decodes to 2025-11-04, which shows the song still circulating on TikTok almost a year later. — [TikTok @poloactivee](https://www.tiktok.com/@poloactivee/video/7568970424743120158)
- According to search-engine snippets, Last.fm has a "Winnie Harlow — nettspend" track page, and a search summary called it a bonus track that came with digital BAFK purchases. I did not open the page. — [Last.fm](https://www.last.fm/music/nettspend/_/Winnie+Harlow)
- The Nettspend Fandom wiki's BAFK page (read via its API) does **not** mention "Winnie Harlow" or the Winnie Version. It looks partly machine-generated: it contains the leftover line "If you want, I can create a full tracklist table" and has no actual tracklist. — [Nettspend Fandom: BAFK](https://nettspend.fandom.com/wiki/Bad_Ass_F*cking_Kid_(Mixtape)); a Fandom site search for "Winnie Harlow" returned no results ([API query](https://nettspend.fandom.com/api.php?action=query&list=search&srsearch=Winnie%20Harlow&format=json))

### Inferences
- Likely sequence: BAFK standard released Dec 6, 2024. The webstore "Winnie Version" went on sale around Dec 6–12 (the SoundCloud uploader's Dec 8 "release_date" may be when the uploader thinks the bonus appeared, but that is unconfirmed). Fans posted "(Bonus Track)" on Dec 12, a SoundCloud rip followed within about an hour, and the Audiomack re-upload came Dec 14. "Archive" in these upload names is a fan label; it does not mean the song was a leak.
- **Duration discrepancy:** Wikipedia lists "Winnie Harlow" at 2:49, but both fan uploads are about 2:17–2:18. Either Wikipedia's figure is wrong, or the fan rips are about 30 s shorter than the webstore file (trimmed intro or outro, or a different edit). Compare your file's length against both figures. This is **unresolved**.
- The track appears to be missing from Spotify-based BPM databases (tunebat, songbpm) and I found no Genius credits page. This fits a webstore-only bonus that was never distributed to streaming services (**Medium**; I did not check DSP catalogs directly).
- Era: this is late-2024, BAFK-era OK production, recorded some time within BAFK's 2020–2024 window. The exact recording date is unknown.

### Gaps
- Exact on-sale date of the Winnie Version on the Interscope store (the store page shows none).
- Whether the song was snippeted on Instagram, TikTok or livestream before release. I found no evidence, and Reddit could not be searched.
- Whether "Winnie Harlow" ever reached Spotify, Apple Music or other streaming services.
- Where the 2:17 vs 2:49 length difference comes from.
- No Nettspend leak-tracker spreadsheet listing this song was found or accessible.

## Listed BPM and key vs. our measurement

### Takeaway
The only site listing found for Nettspend's "Winnie Harlow" is Chordify: **G# minor, 136 BPM**, chords F#, G#m, E7, E. That comes from a search-result snippet, since Chordify's page was Cloudflare-blocked (**Medium**). The key matches our measurement (G# minor). The tempo is about 3% below our 140 BPM, a typical auto-detection error rather than a real disagreement. No tunebat, songbpm or musicstax entry exists, consistent with the song not being on Spotify.

### Cited Findings
- Chordify's "Nettspend - Winnie Harlow" page (URL slug "nettspend-winnie-harlow-bafk") appears in search results with key G# minor, tempo 136 BPM and chords F#, G#m, E7, E. A direct fetch returned HTTP 403 (Cloudflare), so these values come only from the search snippet. — [Chordify](https://chordify.net/chords/nettspend-winnie-harlow-bafk)
- Searches of tunebat, songbpm and musicstax found **no** entry for Nettspend's "Winnie Harlow". Tunebat has other Nettspend tracks (for example "Feeliinuu", "halftime", "Section"), and songbpm has only Abugat's unrelated "WINNIE HARLOW". — [tunebat: Nettspend "Section"](https://tunebat.com/Info/Section-Nettspend/4cYzWnLGs6poIlf3ajWRJv); [SongBPM: Abugat](https://songbpm.com/@abugat/winnie-harlow-dr6YTLdEUW)

### Inferences
- **Key agrees** (G# minor). The Chordify chord set of G#m, F# and E (E7) fits a G# natural-minor or Aeolian loop, i.e. i–bVII–bVI. Chordify's chord names are machine estimates.
- **Tempo:** 136 (Chordify) vs 140 (ours). That is about 50 cents' worth of speed difference, so the +15 cent tuning offset cannot explain it. It is most likely Chordify's beat tracker being slightly off, or picking a different grid, on a half-time trap beat. 140 BPM felt as 70 half-time is the more plausible production tempo, since 140 is a standard trap/rage grid. No site confirms 140 exactly, so treat the 136 figure as low-precision.
- No site mentions the +15 cent tuning. A slight sharp offset in a fan rip could come from OK's sample or synth tuning, or from a speed change somewhere in the fan-upload chain.

### Gaps
- No verified BPM or key from a Spotify-API-based site, because the song is apparently not on Spotify.
- No producer-stated BPM, key or project file information.

## Producer or artist statements (interviews, X, Instagram, livestreams)

### Takeaway
I found **no** public statement from Nettspend or OK specifically about "Winnie Harlow". The only related social posts are from fan accounts (Nettspend Central, archive uploaders, TikTok editors). OK's general background is documented in an Our Generation Music interview.

### Cited Findings
- Nettspend Central, a fan account and not Nettspend himself, announced the bonus track on X on Dec 12, 2024. — [X: @nettcentral](https://x.com/nettcentral/status/1867296239752294716)
- OK's July 2024 Our Generation Music interview covers his Charlotte background, his work with Nettspend, OsamaSon and Glokk40Spaz, and his aim of making "beautiful music". It predates "Winnie Harlow" and does not mention it. — [Our Generation Music](https://ourgenerationmusic.com/featured/wegonebeok-interview-glokk40spaz-nettspend-osamason/)

### Inferences
- The song's lack of press and its webstore-only release suggest it was treated as a fan-service bonus (a reason to buy the $8 digital edition), not a promoted single.
