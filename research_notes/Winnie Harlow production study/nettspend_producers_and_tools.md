# Nettspend's Producers (2023-2026): Credits, Tools, Kits, Cookups and Sound

Research date: 2026-09-29. Method notes: per-track credits were taken mainly from Qobuz album pages (these show label-supplied producer/composer/engineer credits) and Wikipedia; Genius, Pitchfork, Reddit and WhoSampled blocked automated access. YouTube video metadata (titles, channels, dates, descriptions, timestamps) was pulled directly; YouTube transcripts could not be retrieved, so the technique notes below come from titles, descriptions and chapter markers, not from watching the videos. Fan re-uploads of paid, unreleased or leaked audio are deliberately not linked.

## Q1. Who are Nettspend's recurring producers and which songs did each make?

### Takeaway
**ok (wegonebeok)** is his most important producer. He is from Charlotte, NC, and his credited legal name is William (Dale) Minnix (III). He made the breakout "Nothing Like Uuu", "F*ck Swag" and "Withdrawals", 10-11 of the 15 BAFK tracks, and the webstore bonus track "Winnie Harlow".
- **2023:** the early songs came from **zoot** ("drankdrankdrank", "we not like you") and **mag and Xion Orion** ("shine n peace").
- **2025-26:** the sound moved to the Che-adjacent rage crew: **CXO** (Nathaniel Campos, who also mixed and mastered *Early Life Crisis* and *Him*), **Rok/RokOnTheTrack** (Rok Curkovic), **Gyro**, **SKAI**, **LG/Legion**, **Cranes**, **Warren Hunter**, **ss3bby** and **Reklus1ve**.
- **"KwickedOnAllPlats" is not a real credit.** The string is the bio of a small TikTok account ("KWicked on all plats"), and it appears to come from auto-generated aggregator text. Every primary credit I found for "Winnie Harlow" names ok.

### Cited Findings

**"Winnie Harlow": verifying ok vs. "KwickedOnAllPlats"**
- **Wikipedia's tracklist credits ok.** It lists "Winnie Harlow" (2:49) as track 16, a webstore-exclusive bonus track on *Bad Ass F*cking Kid*, produced by **OK**. — [Wikipedia: Bad Ass F*cking Kid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bad_Ass_F*cking_Kid)
- **Interscope sells it as a separate edition.** The "BAD ASS F*CKING KID (WINNIE VERSION)" digital mixtape costs $8, has 16 tracks with "Winnie Harlow" as #16, and is delivered as MP3. The product page lists no producer credits. — [Interscope store](https://interscope.com/products/bad-ass-f-cking-kid-winnie-version-digital-mixtape)
- **Release timing:** fan account Nettspend Central posted "WINNIE HARLOW (Bonus Track)" on 2024-12-12 (date decoded from the post ID), six days after BAFK came out on 2024-12-06. — [X: @nettcentral](https://x.com/nettcentral/status/1867296239752294716)
- **Fan re-uploads and posts title the song "nettspend - winnie harlow (prod ok)".** A remake channel's upload of the vocal-less instrumental says "Prod OK", and a later upload says "Prod wegonbeok". I'm not linking those rips. — [X: @willnotdunnyet](https://x.com/willnotdunnyet/status/1873139321722814633); [SoundCloud station title "(prod ok)"](https://soundcloud.com/discover/sets/track-stations:1982459479)
- **Where "KwickedOnAllPlats" comes from:** the TikTok account @_kwicked (display name "rey"; about 258 followers and 67 videos when checked) has the bio "ig: _kwicked / KWicked on all plats". Web-search AI summaries turned that string into a "producer" of "Winnie Harlow" through TikTok's auto-generated "Winnie Harlow Nettspend Instrumental" discover page. No label, DSP, Wikipedia or fan-archive credit supports it. — [TikTok @_kwicked](https://www.tiktok.com/@_kwicked); [TikTok discover page](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/winnie-harlow-nettspend-instrumental)
- **I found no DSP listing for "Winnie Harlow".** An iTunes Search API query returned no results, which fits it being a webstore-only bonus track. So I could not check Spotify or Apple credits. (Checked via the iTunes Search API, not a page link.)

### Inferences
- **Two production eras.** Credits suggest the 2023-2024 "jerk/angelic" era (zoot, mag, ok, Reklus1ve) and the 2025-2026 "rage/distorted" era (CXO, Rok, Gyro, SKAI, LG, Cranes, Warren Hunter), with ok as the bridge ("<3 me").
- **CXO and "natecxo" are probably the same person.** Remake channels title videos "How Natecxo & Rok make..." ([MSFT](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xS_wovnd95k)), and the credited name is Nathaniel Campos. This is a strong inference, not confirmed.
- **The "Winnie Harlow" beat should be modeled as an ok (wegonebeok) production in his BAFK-era style.** Under this reading, "KwickedOnAllPlats" is a data-quality artifact of AI-summarized TikTok discover pages.

### Gaps
- **No authoritative producer credit for "Winnie Harlow"** from a label or DSP metadata feed. It isn't on Qobuz, the iTunes Search API returned nothing, and Genius blocked automated access. The OK credit rests on Wikipedia plus consistent fan metadata.
- **"Say Please" is unresolved:** Methboiswag (Wikipedia) vs. OK (Qobuz, composer Fredrik Willert).
- **ok vs. OTW on "<3 me":** I couldn't confirm which of the two composers (William Dale Minnix III or Reginald Bullock) is which producer. Minnix = ok is well supported; Bullock = OTW is only a guess.

## Q2. What DAW and tools does each producer use? Do they sell kits or presets, and are any stems or project files public?

### Takeaway
There is almost no first-party, citable documentation of any Nettspend producer's plugin list.
- **DAW:** FL Studio is the working assumption for ok. It rests on a fan description of his live beatmaking stream, plus nearly every "how ok/CXO/Rok made X" recreation being done in FL Studio.
- **Plugins:** the names that come up are Serum/Serum 2, Omnisphere, Spire, Roland Zenology, Purity, GMS, ACE, ShaperBox, and FL stock Fruity WaveShaper + Fruity Limiter for distortion and clipping. All of these come from fan remakes, not the producers.
- **Kits:** "ok"-branded drum kits circulate, but I couldn't verify an official ok storefront. The kit names appear on third-party resellers and leak sites.
- **Project files:** no official stems or FLPs of any Nettspend beat are public. Many fan remake FLPs sit behind Patreon or Discord like-goals.

## Q3. What producer interviews, cookups and "how X made Y" breakdowns exist, and what techniques do they show?

### Takeaway
**First-party material:**
- zoot's On The Radar beat breakdown of "We not like you" (Oct 2025).
- ok's OGM interview (Jul 2024) and many archived IG-live cookups.
- Trending Raps "Flipping Packs" episodes with Cranes and Reklus1ve.

**Third-party recreations:** these cover "Winnie Harlow" (FD Remakes, SonMaid), "Shut Up", "Project Pat", "Nothing Like Uuu", "shine n peace", "drankdrankdrank", "hopscotch" and most *Early Life Crisis* tracks. A consistent method runs through all of it:
- **Melody:** a chord pad or sampled and chopped loop, plus a "catchy lead" and choir/vocal textures, often from a pitched or chopped sample (e.g. "Boo'd Up" on "Nothing Like Uuu").
- **Drums:** claps, hats and snares, with FX one-shots such as glass breaks and lasers.
- **808s:** a distorted "808 sauce".
- **Master:** distortion and clipping on the master.

I found no Sound Hokage video on Nettspend.

## Q4. What do journalists and producers say defines the sound of Nettspend's beats?

### Takeaway
Press describes two layers:
- **2023-24 (jerk/"angelic" era):** euphoric, trance-like, glimmering or "arctic-shiver" synths, loose piano, frantic snares and handclaps, pitched vocal layers, with "demonic synths" set against "angelic textures".
- **BAFK and ELC era:** oversaturated, clipped, distorted 808s ("bass that makes speakers sound broken"), bit-crushed samples (Salem, Grimes), witch house and cloud rap, then harder rage for ELC (panic-alarm synths, metallic percussion, Crystal Castles-like arpeggios).

Heavy Auto-Tune and very short songs (often under two minutes) run through all of it.

### Cited Findings
- **The FADER** (via Wikipedia): his production uses "distorted 808s, loose piano riffs, and glimmering synths". — [Wikipedia: Nettspend](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nettspend)
- **Pitchfork** (via Wikipedia): jerk means "pitched-up, layered vocals and bass that will make your speakers sound broken". — [Wikipedia: Nettspend](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nettspend)
- **The NYT** called him "post-post-rage", and most of his tracks run under two minutes. — [Wikipedia: Nettspend](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nettspend)
- **"Shine N Peace":** Colin Joyce (The FADER) heard "a flurry of snares and a slipstream of trance synths", calling it "blissed out, blistered, and relentlessly euphoric". Vivian Medithi noted the "frenzied groove and unceasing handclaps". — [Wikipedia: Shine N Peace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shine_N_Peace)
- **i-D:** "arctic-shiver synths" and rapidly fluttering drums with an arrhythmic effect. zoot and ok pair "demonic synths" with "angelic textures". — [i-D](https://i-d.co/article/nettspend-debut-album-profile-interview-2024/)
- **BAFK in general:** described as "oversaturated vocals, AutoTune, clipping, and distorted 808s", across rage, trap, witch house and cloud rap. — [Wikipedia BAFK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bad_Ass_F*cking_Kid)
- **BAFK track notes (Wikipedia):**
  - "Leader" uses "an old Salem song bit-crushed to oblivion".

- **ok on OsamaSon's *Jump Out*:** the FADER notes "discordant pan flutes" and "wobbling 808s" that are "perfectly translucent", in uncluttered, precisely mixed arrangements. — [The FADER](https://www.thefader.com/2025/01/31/osamason-jump-out-interview-leaks-rage-rap-opium)

### Inferences
- **For a "Winnie Harlow"-era (BAFK, ok) recreation,** the press and tutorial evidence points to:
  - Angelic or heavenly pad and choir layers, possibly sample-based.
  - A bright lead.
  - Sparse, crisp claps and hats with FX one-shots.
  - A loud, saturated 808.
  - Overall clipping or saturation on the master.

  The harsher panic-alarm, metallic rage palette belongs to the later CXO/Rok/LG *Early Life Crisis* era.
